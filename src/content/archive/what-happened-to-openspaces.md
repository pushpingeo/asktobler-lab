Well it’s been six months since the [Ordnance Survey (OS)](https://web.archive.org/web/2007/http://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/oswebsite/) announced a beta of their Openspaces API and we’ve heard no more? Attendees at the [UK Geospatial Mashup event](https://web.archive.org/web/2007/http://www.agi.org.uk/pooled/articles/BF_EVENTART/view.asp?Q=BF_EVENTART_214953) held at the OS last October where treated to a sneak preview – it looked good, a Google Maps like API using OS data (slides are online at the [OGC](https://web.archive.org/web/2007/https://portal.opengeospatial.org/files/?artifact_id=18199)). This is just the kind of service the UK public deserve, and in my opinion, the type of service the OS needs to start providing if they are to fully capitalize on future opportunities and compete in a fast changing market place.

Chinese whispers at the Mashup event last October suggested it would go live within six months…or not at all. Hopefully it’s just around the corner, pending execution, and their [CTO joining Google](https://web.archive.org/web/2007/http://www.edparsons.com/?p=448) was just a coincidence.
