The pace of change in AI and geospatial technology is staggering—and it's not just about better models or more data. It’s also about how we *understand* the implications of these systems: ethically, practically, and geopolitically.

Here’s a short reading list for those curious to explore the bigger picture as well as emerging applications in #GeoAI.

---

## 🌐 Big Picture + Ethics

**📖 The Digitalist Papers** What would the *Federalist Papers* look like if written today? This thoughtful series brings together voices from law, technology, economics, and political science—exploring how digital technologies, including AI, are reshaping institutions and society. 🔗 [digitalistpapers.com](http://digitalistpapers.com)

**🤖 The Alignment Forum** AI alignment is the task of ensuring that AI systems reliably reflect human intentions, values, and constraints. This forum offers deep dives on technical alignment, safety, and governance—critical reading as models become more capable and autonomous. 🔗 [alignmentforum.org](http://alignmentforum.org)

---

## 🛰️ Geospatial AI: Literature and Research

**📚 Academic Review of GeoAI** This open-access paper provides a comprehensive review of GeoAI: its evolution, key techniques, and current research frontiers. A good starting point if you’re looking to understand where the field is heading. 🔗 [ScienceDirect: GeoAI Review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1569843225000159)

**🧭 GIS Co-Pilot by Penn State** A fascinating look at what an "autonomous GIS agent" could be—using AI to guide spatial analysis in QGIS. Applied, forward-looking, and open for collaboration. 🔗 [Penn State GIScience Lab](https://giscience.psu.edu/gis-copilot-towards-an-autonomous-gis-agent-for-spatial-analysis/)

**🕵️ Agents and Model Context Protocol (MCP)** AI Agents are the #McKinsey dream by which all employees in an organisation are replaced with LLM powered bots, MCP will enable the agents to drive the tools (and you can get started with GeoServer) 🔗 [GeoServer MCP](https://github.com/mahdin75/geoserver-mcp) …and ⭐️ pick on this subject over at Stadia Maps: <https://stadiamaps.com/news/ai-tools-for-api-companies-ai-needs-context/>

## 🏗️ Big Tech & GeoAI

**🖥️ NVIDIA in AECO + Geospatial** From Earth-scale simulations to 3D cities, NVIDIA is bringing its full-stack compute power to the AECO and geospatial sectors. Great overview by Sean Young on how they're accelerating use cases. 🔗 [How NVIDIA Accelerates AECO & Geospatial](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-nvidia-accelerates-aeco-geospatial-full-stack-solutions-young-rlixe/)

**🗺️ Google Research: Geospatial Reasoning** Building on tools like Earth Engine and Maps, Google is now exploring how generative AI can support deeper geospatial reasoning using multiple foundation models. 🔗 [**Google Research Blog**](https://research.google/blog/geospatial-reasoning-unlocking-insights-with-generative-ai-and-multiple-foundation-models/)

🩺 **A Foundation Model for Geospatial Inference** Some insight on considerations when building a foundation model from Google Research (thx Kevin Holohan) . 🔗 [Google Health AI](https://research.google/blog/insights-into-population-dynamics-a-foundation-model-for-geospatial-inference/)
