Another rainy weekend proved a good excuse to sit down and put some theory into practice…

The theory goes…take a GPS enabled mobile phone; some beta software from Nokia; a handful of HTML, Javascript and PHP goodness; and you have all the components for a dynamic tracking web service and mapping website?

Well, the theory works! I’ve put a demonstration together at the following link:

[Mobile Phone Tracking with a Nokia N95 Demonstration](https://web.archive.org/web/2008/http://gisconsultancy.com/mws/mws.html)

Given all the current hype regarding [some telecomunications technology](https://web.archive.org/web/2008/http://blip.tv/file/719841); I’m puzzled by the apparent lack of interest in the mobile web server. Sure, there are a few pieces to still fall into place, but if the mobile web server’s graduation from Nokia research labs, results in widespread deployment, it must have a profound impact on the web?
